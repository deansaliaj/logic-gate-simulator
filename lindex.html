How to use: Select a gate from the toolbar and click the canvas to place it. Click the blue output dot (right side) of a gate, then the input dot (left side) of another to wire them. Click Input nodes to toggle between 0 and 1. Use Pan mode to drag the canvas, and scroll to zoom.

Supports AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR and XOR gates. Auto-generates truth tables for up to 4 inputs. Built for EEE / digital electronics students.
